The estate

The ideal place for a family holiday.

Es Rafal Roig is a traditional Mallorcan stone estate a few kilometres from Manacor, surrounded by olive trees, gardens and countryside. It has nine independent apartments and studios, each with its own entrance, kitchen and terrace, plus a swimming pool, a children’s play area and shared spaces to enjoy as a family.

For families

Apartments for up to 4 guests, a play area and cots on request.

Swimming pool

Outdoor pool with sun loungers, surrounded by olive trees and countryside views.

Independence

Every apartment has a private entrance, its own kitchen and terrace.

Great location

A few kilometres from Manacor and 25 km from s’Albufera Natural Park.
